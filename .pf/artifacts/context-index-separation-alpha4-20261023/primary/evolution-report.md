# Evolution from the alpha4 task

Separate observation from maintenance at composition boundaries. Keep a single
explicit maintenance owner, genuine narrow backend port and cold constructors.
Batch native transactions require sequential fresh plans and confirmed audit
success before their IDs enter one final selected indexing pass. Audit event
deduplication and required notification delivery have distinct retry semantics.
Source unavailability must never masquerade as an empty authorized corpus.
An explicitly maintained empty corpus must not masquerade as a missing index;
schema-only or inconsistent storage must never masquerade as a fresh corpus.
Retain rejected candidates, fault reproduction and exact evidence layers.

Periodic scheduler registration, additional SQL/vector adapters and a RAG pipeline
remain separate future work. Existing derived caches may require one explicit
full pass to establish new root/metadata identities; context never performs it.
Installed-device, actual connected-host and native POSIX qualification are not
claimed by source/archive or fixture stdio checks. Retained SQLite warnings need
their own attribution and scope; this delivery does not claim they were fixed.
